ECS rules NOT yet implemented in ecs-go
=======================================

Source: every Fixer/Sniff referenced across ecs-src config/set/* and
src/Config/Level/* (psr12, common, clean-code, standalone-line, laravel,
doctrine-annotations, and the Array/ControlStructures/Docblock/Spaces levels).

Totals: 197 rules configured in ECS - 45 covered here = ~152 not yet done.
(psr12-left.txt tracks the PSR-12 subset specifically; this is the whole ECS
surface, most of which is beyond PSR-12: docblocks, arrays, cleanup, etc.)

Note - a few below are already covered under a newer PHP-CS-Fixer name:
  NoSpacesInsideParenthesisFixer   -> we have SpacesInsideParenthesesFixer
